# Privacy Policy for Soma

## 1. Information We Collect
Soma does not collect, record, or track any personal information. No data related to user identity, personal profiles, or app usage is collected, stored, or analyzed.

## 2. Use of Data & Third-Party Libraries
The App does not implement user-tracking analytics or advertising frameworks. It may utilize third-party libraries (such as Google ML Kit) to enable on-device translation capabilities. These libraries may collect technical and operational diagnostic data (such as processing performance and crash logs) solely to maintain service reliability. This diagnostic data never contains translated texts, user inputs, or personally identifiable information (PII).

## 3. Data Storage
The App may provide functionality to save articles, bookmarks, or reading preferences locally on your device for offline access and improved performance. All saved content remains exclusively on your local device storage and is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any external server.

## 4. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user or device data with third parties. No private data is ever transmitted from your device.

## 5. External Content
The App retrieves and displays content from external platforms via public APIs and web links. All external content is governed by the respective terms of service and privacy policies of the host sources (including the Wikimedia Foundation). The App exercises no control over third-party policies.

## 6. Disclaimer
The App and its developer assume no responsibility for the accuracy, validity, completeness, or ongoing availability of the articles and external materials displayed. All content is fetched directly from Wikipedia and public endpoints and does not necessarily represent the opinions or positions of the developer.

## 7. Device Permissions
The App strictly requests network connectivity permissions (`android.permission.INTERNET`) necessary to fetch articles and assets. No sensitive permissions (such as contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location) are requested.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be revised periodically. Any updates will take effect upon publication within the application or its official distribution channels.

## 9. Contact
For questions regarding this policy or the application, please open an issue or inquiry via the official project repository.
